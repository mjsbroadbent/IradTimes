headline: Tulparcoin name case to 16 November
date: 2026-10-15
section: In brief
kind: brief
issue: 1577

The Talas District Court adjourned the Talas Commercial Development Group’s request for an injunction against the Tulparcoin name until 16 November. Judge Asel Mamytbekova asked both sides whether a cryptocurrency is a “financial service” at all. The coin stays blocked by Attelkom and traded at about three US cents from abroad.
